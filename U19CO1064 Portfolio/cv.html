Project Experience

AI Powered Sickle Cell Disease Alert System

Area: Edge AI

An offline handheld device designed for rural areas in Zaria where Primary Healthcare Centres may not have the necessary equipment to measure haemoglobin levels of sickle cell disease patients. The system captures a photograph of the lower eyelid and uses both a regression task and a classification task to provide results.

The classification produces one of three outcomes: Non-Anemic, Moderate, or Severe. In the case of a severe result, the system is designed to alert Ahmadu Bello University Teaching Hospital so that blood bags can be prepared before the patient arrives.

Technologies: ESP32-CAM, ESP-WROOM-32, SIM800L, VL53LO/1XV2 Distance Sensor, ROHS 2.8" 240×RGB×320 Display and NeoPixel 8 LED Ring Light.

My Role: Hardware Integration

Hausa Movie Review Sentiment Analysis

Area: Natural Language Processing

A sentiment analysis system that analyses movie reviews written in Hausa or in a combination of Hausa and English. The system classifies reviews into positive, neutral, and negative sentiments.

Two Kaggle datasets were merged to improve model accuracy and include both pure Hausa and Engausa reviews. Feature vectors were extracted using techniques including BERTopic, Model2Vec and Potion. The classifiers used were FastKAN and Support Vector Machine, with the best-performing classifier and extracted features deployed through Gradio.

My Role: Team Lead

Learning Management System with Smart Feedback Analysis

Area: Natural Language Processing

A learning management system designed to provide lecturers with feedback reports based on student feedback regarding lectures and assignments. The system is intended to save lecturers' time while providing an avenue for quicker responses to topics and concerns raised by students.

Technologies: Fine-tuned pretrained AI model on an educational dataset, Figma for UI design, Firebase for user authentication, and frontend development and deployment for the prototype phase.

My Role: Team Lead
