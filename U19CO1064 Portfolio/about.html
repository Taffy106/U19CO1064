About Me

Who I Am

I am a final-year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, originally from Borno State and raised in Abuja, Nigeria. I chose Computer Engineering because it is a field that constantly evolves, challenges the way we think, and gives me an outlet for my creativity. Over the past three years, the projects I have worked on have deepened my interest in both the technical and creative sides of engineering.

I am naturally curious, creative, compassionate, and meticulous. I enjoy seeing how much there is still to learn and allowing that curiosity to influence both my engineering and my art. My interest in Artificial Intelligence comes from the idea that machines can be taught to develop intelligence and from the possibility of combining that technology with creativity.

I am particularly motivated by problems that affect people who are genuinely struggling. My interest in healthcare comes from seeing lifelong health challenges affect both myself and people around me. I want to use what I learn to create technology that can make even a small, meaningful difference in someone's life.

My Career Goals

I aspire to become a Computer Engineer specialising in Healthcare Artificial Intelligence. I want to explore how intelligent systems can be designed and applied to real healthcare problems, particularly where technology can support people who may otherwise have limited access to effective care.

My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of AI, engineering, and art. I want to build systems that are technically meaningful while still leaving room for creativity, human connection, and thoughtful design. I hope to continue learning, developing practical solutions, and eventually contribute to healthcare technologies that can make a real difference.

My Values

  • Curiosity - I value asking questions, exploring ideas, and staying open to learning.
  • Compassion - I want the things I build to have a meaningful human purpose.
  • Creativity - I believe engineering is also a form of creation, and I want to bring imagination into the way I solve problems.
