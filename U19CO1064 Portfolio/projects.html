NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

HAUSA MOVIE REVIEW SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

Movie reviews written in Hausa or in a combination of Hausa and English are analysed for their sentiments. These include positive, neutral and negative classifications.

Technologies: Two Kaggle datasets were merged to improve model accuracy and to include both pure Hausa and Engausa reviews. Feature vectors were extracted using techniques including BERTopic, Model2Vec and Potion. The classifiers used were FastKAN and Support Vector Machine. The best-performing classifier with the best extracted features was deployed using Gradio.

EDGE AI

AI POWERED SICKLE CELL DISEASE ALERT SYSTEM

This is an offline handheld device designed for rural areas in Zaria where Primary Healthcare Centres may not have the necessary equipment to measure haemoglobin levels of sickle cell disease patients.

The device captures a photograph of the lower eyelid and uses both a regression task and a classification task to provide results. The classification produces one of three outcomes: Non-Anemic, Moderate, or Severe.

In the case of a severe result, the system is designed to alert Ahmadu Bello University Teaching Hospital so that blood bags can be prepared before the patient arrives. This device is a baseline with room for further improvement to cater for other crises associated with sickle cell disease.

Technologies: ESP32-CAM, ESP-WROOM-32, SIM800L, VL53LO/1XV2 Distance Sensor, ROHS 2.8" 240×RGB×320 Display and NeoPixel 8 LED Ring Light.
